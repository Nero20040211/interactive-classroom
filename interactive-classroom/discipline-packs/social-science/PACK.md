# Social Science Discipline Pack

Pack ID: `social-science`

Status: **validated-native**

Use for sociology, communication, policy, management, economics-as-social-science and related evidence/decision domains.

## Native learning grammar

1. separate description, association, causal claim and normative judgment;
2. make causal assumptions visible;
3. compare study designs and limits;
4. use PBL only after evidence/inference boundaries have been taught.

## Native widgets

- `causal-dag-lab`
- `evidence-matrix-lab`
- supporting `data-lab`, discussion and PBL.

## Quality gate

- A causal DAG has one exposure and one outcome, valid directed edges, no directed cycles, explicit adjustment candidates/valid sets, and a causal-claim boundary.
- The DAG is an assumption model, not empirical proof.
- Evidence-matrix rows name design, evidence type, claim scope, limitation and source.
- Observational association must not be phrased as deterministic causal identification without an appropriate design/assumption contract.


## animation profile

Causal/data-generating animation must show assumptions and uncertainty; DAG arrows are not automatically empirical mechanisms.
