Pack ID: `biology`

Status: **validated-native**

Preferred: animated diagram, annotation, genetics-lab, data-lab. Separate structure, process, function and evidence. Genetics labs must declare genotype→phenotype mapping and probabilities.

## Pack quality gate

- Knowledge explanation remains primary.
- Use only subject-native notation and declare assumptions.

- Core offline, accessibility, MathML and citation contracts still apply.
