# Pedagogy Reference

Use this reference when designing the learning loop, feedback, remediation, or mastery checks.

## Core loop

A strong scene often follows:

1. **Elicit** — prediction, recall, or explanation before the answer.
2. **Expose** — surface the learner's assumption or choice.
3. **Respond** — give the smallest useful explanation/hint.
4. **Rebuild** — ask the learner to apply or explain again.
5. **Transfer** — change the surface form or context.
6. **Check** — test the objective directly.

Do not turn every scene into a quiz. Alternate explanation, exploration, application, and reflection.

## Feynman-style explain-back

Use explain-back when conceptual understanding matters.

Prompt structure:

- “用自己的话解释 X，不要复述定义。”
- “给一个你自己构造的例子。”
- “这个解释在哪种情况下会失效？”

Offline HTML cannot reliably grade arbitrary prose. Provide a checklist such as:

- names the mechanism;
- distinguishes cause from correlation;
- includes one concrete example;
- states one boundary condition.

Offer a button to reveal the checklist only after the learner writes/thinks.

## Socratic hint ladder

For difficult questions, use staged hints:

- Hint 1: redirect attention to the relevant variable or premise.
- Hint 2: give a partial relationship, not the answer.
- Hint 3: provide a worked analogous case.
- Reveal: show the answer and explain the reasoning.

Track hint use locally if useful, but never shame the learner for using hints.

## Misconception branches

Important distractors should encode plausible misconceptions. Feedback must say why the choice is tempting and what distinction fixes it.

Bad:

- “错误，请重试。”

Better:

- “这个选项把‘更容易被注意’等同于‘更容易被相信’。注意力和可信度是不同机制。先比较两个变量，再重试。”

## Cognitive load

Prefer:

- one main idea per scene;
- progressive disclosure;
- concise labels near the relevant visual;
- optional deep-dive panels for detail;
- no simultaneous animation competing with a quiz.

## Mastery evidence

Map every major objective to at least one observable behavior:

- explain a mechanism;
- classify cases;
- predict model behavior;
- choose a justified intervention;
- interpret a graph;
- detect a flawed inference;
- transfer to a new example.

A final “完成课程” button is not mastery evidence.
