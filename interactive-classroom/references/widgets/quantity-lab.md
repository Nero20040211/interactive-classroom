# quantity-lab widget

Use for physics/engineering dimensional reasoning. Quantities require unit + dimension. The relation requires `lhsDimension` / `rhsDimension`; show that dimensional agreement is a necessary check, not proof of physical truth.

## adjustable quantities

Quantities may be learner-adjustable using `adjustable/min/max/step`. A deterministic `model.kind` computes derived quantities locally. See `../interactive-experiments.md`.
