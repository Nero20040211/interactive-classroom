# Widget Contract — `space-geometry-lab`

Owner pack: `mathematics`.

Purpose: train 3D positional reasoning in a named cuboid model rather than trusting a 2D drawing alone.

Canonical relation IDs:
- parallel
- intersect
- skew
- contained
- perpendicular
- line-plane-parallel
- line-plane-intersect
- plane-plane-parallel
- plane-plane-intersect

A scene needs at least three relation questions. Every question supplies two named objects, a deterministic answer, answer options, and feedback that refers to definitions/theorems rather than apparent drawing angle.

The canonical cuboid uses A,B,C,D and A1,B1,C1,D1. The SVG is an explanatory projection; relations are determined by the model, not by pixel slope.
