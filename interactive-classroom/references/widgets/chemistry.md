# chemistry widget

Use structured compounds and atom counts for balancing practice. Coefficients may change; compound subscripts/atom composition may not be altered to force balance. Mark `expectBalanced` when strict atom-conservation validation should apply.

## balancing experiment

Use `adjustableCoefficients:true` for stoichiometric balancing practice. Only coefficients may change; compound atom structure/subscripts remain fixed. Atom conservation must recalculate from structured atoms after every adjustment.
