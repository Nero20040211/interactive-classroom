# Widget Contract — `solid-geometry-lab`

Owner pack: `mathematics`.

Purpose: let learners vary geometric parameters and observe volume/surface-area consequences for common solids.

Canonical kinds:
- prism
- pyramid
- cylinder
- cone
- sphere
- frustum-pyramid
- frustum-cone

Each shape needs:
- stable `id` and `label`;
- adjustable `parameters` with min/max/step/unit;
- structured MathML `formula` when a formula is shown;
- at least one control-variable experiment at the scene or shape level.

Runtime results are deterministic numerical models, not CAD/measurement software. Diagrams are schematic and not necessarily to scale.
